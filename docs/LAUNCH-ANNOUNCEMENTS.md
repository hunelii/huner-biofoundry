## Short press-style announcement

### Huner Biofoundry launches website introducing its living-systems approach

Huner Biofoundry has launched a new website presenting its work with living systems for food, fragrance and biological raw materials.

The website features the Living Foundry, an interactive experience in which visitors alter environmental conditions and observe a delayed generative response. A three-dimensional fungal-cell cutaway offers a schematic view of structures within a living system.

The experience reflects Huner's statement, “Making with life, for life,” and introduces the application directions behind its work. The simulations and cell model are illustrative, rather than experimental results or reconstructions of a Huner organism.
